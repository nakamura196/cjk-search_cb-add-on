Search Options

Search finds the words you type anywhere in {{ fields | join: ", " }}, in any language, including Chinese, Japanese and Korean:

  • All words: separate words with spaces to find items that contain every one of them, e.g. map 1911.
  • Part of a word: Chinese, Japanese and Korean text matches anywhere inside a title or description, e.g. 大學 finds 東京帝國大學. The last English word matches as a beginning, e.g. photo finds photograph.
  • Variant forms: old and new forms of kanji (圖 / 図), hiragana and katakana, and full-width and half-width letters are treated as the same.
  • Small typos: English words of five or more letters allow one wrong letter.
